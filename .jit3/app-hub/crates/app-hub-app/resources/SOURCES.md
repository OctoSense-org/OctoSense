# Application identity

Canonical artwork: `../assets/icon.svg`, declared by `../listing.json`. It is a
shopping bag with the OctoSense logo on its front, drawn as SVG geometry.
Concept images and prompts, including the earlier Octo Bloom concept, live in
OctoSense, `phone/docs/design/app-hub/icon-exploration/`.

Preview entries carry no artwork of their own. They draw Makepad's shared
`AppIcon` identities for News, Maps, Photos, Camera, Mail and Sheets, with the
artwork of the active desktop style. This crate ships no copies of those icons.
