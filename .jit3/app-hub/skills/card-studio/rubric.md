# Card rubric (default)

Use the app's own rubric from its `AGENT.md` when it has one; this is the
fallback.

- **Legible at a glance.** The headline reads in under a second at the
  glance size: large enough, high contrast, not crowded. Nothing is cut,
  squeezed or hidden.
- **One hierarchy.** One headline, then supporting lines in a clearly lower
  weight. No two elements compete for first place.
- **Balanced.** Consistent margins; no large empty region next to a crowded
  one; aligned left edges.
- **Grounded.** Every fact on the card comes from its data. No loading,
  empty or failed state is showing when data was given; no em dash where a
  value should be.
- **Reads as this app's card.** Its subject and voice are the app's (for
  News: what happened, from whom), recognizable next to other apps' cards.
- **Fits every size.** The glance tile shows the essential content without
  scrolling; phone and desktop use their room without stretching thin.
