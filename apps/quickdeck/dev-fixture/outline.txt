The science
	Charged particles stream from the Sun
	Earth's magnetic field funnels them to the poles
	Oxygen glows green and red; nitrogen adds blue and purple
Best months to look
	September to March, on clear dark nights
	High latitudes: Tromso, Fairbanks, Yellowknife
	Check the Kp index and the cloud forecast
Field checklist
	Tripod and spare batteries
	Headlamp with a red mode
	Warm layers and a thermos
	Leave no trace
